# Client and Goals
Up: [[00 Start Here]]

## Direction
- A **secured file management system** able to hold legal documents, in a more enterprise-ready file-system format.
- Code **refactor and adaptability**: the client's real roles and functions override the thesis role list; roles or functions may be removed or reshaped entirely.
- The client has **its own server** and wants the system able to run on it, with a safe way to build a **fresh, empty database** there.
- Deadline: **first week of December 2026** (demo Fri 4 Dec).

## How the work runs
- Input arrives through **weekly interviews**; one interview never gives complete information for a role, so answers accumulate ([[08 Open Questions]]).
- The developer is sometimes available only half a day, so work is cut into small finishable pieces ([[09 Plan to December Demo]]).
- The plan is also kept in a Google Drive folder; this vault is the Obsidian copy.

## Problem context (from the thesis wiki)
ORRA is a long-established construction and architecture firm. Files sit in folders on a cloud drive, schedules are Excel with coloured cells, attendance and payroll are encoded by hand. See the thesis wiki note *The Problem at ORRA*.

## Sign-in and realtime (under evaluation)
Google sign-in (Neon Managed Better Auth) for more secure, reliable login, and Neon Realtime for live notifications. Question 14 asks whether Google company accounts suit all staff, including site crews ([[08 Open Questions]]).
