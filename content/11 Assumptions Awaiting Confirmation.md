# Assumptions Awaiting Confirmation
Up: [[00 Start Here]]

1. **Project Management is a service on the Construction type**, not a separate project type.
2. **A DCO role exists** (separate from Admin and Site Personnel).
3. **PM-only approvals** for materials and RFIs; Admin may override with a written reason.
4. **Outside-geofence clock-ins are flagged, not blocked.**
5. **Three confidentiality levels** (Internal, Confidential, Restricted); contracts and payroll are Restricted.
6. Office clock-in as one button (new, if ORRA confirms).
7. Payroll deductions are placeholders until ORRA confirms the rules.
8. **Google Drive is the file repository**: ORRA has a dedicated 1 TB plan on a single Google account; Orra-ERP uploads as that account (OAuth), encrypts Restricted files, and is the only normal way in ([[13 Google Drive Integration]]).

Each maps to a question in [[08 Open Questions]].
